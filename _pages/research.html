Overview of my research agenda toward secure and accessible digital systems for all users.

My research focuses on security and privacy in modern and emerging digital technologies, with the overarching goal of ensuring that these technologies remain secure and accessible for all users. I investigate overlooked security and privacy risks that can arise when technologies fail to account for users with diverse abilities and interaction needs, as well as risks introduced by the increasingly rich capabilities of emerging technologies. Through this interconnected work, I develop approaches to identify, understand, and mitigate these risks to help make digital systems more secure and accessible.
