  <p>Howdy!</p>

  <p>

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and a Mobil Aggies Future Faculty Fellow (F3) at Texas A&M University, under the supervision of Prof. Nitesh Saxena at the <a href="https://spies.engr.tamu.edu/" target="_blank">SPIES Lab</a>.
  </p>

  <p>
I began my Ph.D. in Spring 2022, and my research focuses on security and privacy in modern and emerging digital technologies, with the overarching goal of ensuring that these technologies remain secure and accessible for all users. In particular, I investigate the often-overlooked connection between accessibility and security by examining how design limitations can make systems inaccessible to users with diverse abilities and interaction needs, preventing them from effectively accessing or understanding security-critical information and making it difficult to recognize threats. I investigate how these limitations can create vulnerabilities that attackers can exploit, disproportionately affecting these users while also extending their consequences to the broader user population. I develop systematic frameworks to identify these limitations, analyze their security and privacy consequences, and guide their detection and mitigation before they can cause harm. I also investigate security and privacy challenges introduced by emerging interaction technologies, particularly extended reality (XR), where rich sensing capabilities that enable enhanced user experiences can also unintentionally reveal sensitive information about users and their environments. To address these challenges, I develop mechanisms that mitigate information leakage and enable secure, accessible, and seamless interactions with these technologies.
      </p>

  <p>
I have published several papers in leading, top-tier venues, including <b style="color:blue;">ACM CCS</b> (three papers), <b style="color:blue;">The Web Conference (WWW)</b>, and <b style="color:blue;">USENIX Security</b>, with one receiving a <b style="color:blue;">Distinguished Paper Award</b> at ACM CCS 2025. In addition, I have published in respected journals and contributed to book chapters. I also have several papers currently under review at top-tier venues and additional projects in progress, with planned submissions to leading conferences and journals. My research has also been featured in national and international media, including <i>Newsweek</i>, <i>MSN</i>, <i>Yahoo! Tech</i>, and <i>The Indian Express</i>, and has been recognized with the <b style="color:blue;">Graduate Research Excellence Award</b> from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Texas A&M University.
</p>
